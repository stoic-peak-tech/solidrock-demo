Our mission

To provide rescue, rehabilitation, and compassionate care for horses and dogs, while fostering hope, healing, and personal growth through Christ-centered programs.

Adopt

Bring one home

When animals are ready, we post them on our Facebook page and open applications. Here is how it goes.

  1. Watch for an announcementWe share application links when a new group is ready.
  2. Submit an applicationApplications are reviewed in the order we receive them.
  3. Our board reviews itWe contact you if your application is accepted.

Serious inquiries only

Every animal here has already been through enough. We match each one carefully, so please apply only if you are ready for the commitment.

Available animals will appear on this page. For now, follow our Facebook page for the latest.
